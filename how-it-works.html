How it works

Three calm steps between you and a scam.

Scammers win by rushing people. Every part of Pause is designed to slow the moment down and bring in a second opinion.

I.

Share it

Press and hold any text or email, tap Share, then Pause. Scan a QR code. Or let Pause screen unknown callers first.

II.

Pause checks it

Our AI reads the wording, checks the link and the sender, and compares it with confirmed reports on Scam Radar.

III.

You get a plain verdict

Safe, Be careful, or Scam, with the reasons and one clear next step. If it's high-risk, your Guardian can be told.

I. The check

One big word. Three reasons. One next step.

No scores to decode. Pause tells you what it found and exactly what to do, like "type sunpass.com yourself" instead of tapping the link.

  • Texts, emails, links, QR codes, and calls
  • Reading sizes up to Largest, set on day one
  • Report to Scam Radar in one tap
II. Family Circle

Your family in the loop, never in your inbox.

Choose a Guardian, like a son or daughter. They're told only when something is high-risk, so parents keep their independence.

  • That a scam was blocked
  • That a risky payment was flagged
  • Never your messages or calls
  • Never your bank balance
III. Family Code Word

A word an AI voice can't copy.

Voices can now be cloned from a short video clip. Pause screens unknown callers, shows you what they said, and reminds you to ask for your family's word.

  • Set together, while everyone is calm
  • Hold to reveal, never shown by accident
  • Call screening powered by a carrier partner
IV. Money Guard

A second look at the payments scammers ask for.

Connected read-only through Plaid. Money Guard flags gift-card runs, first-time wires, and crypto ATM withdrawals, then walks you through what to do.

  • Read-only: Pause can never move your money
  • We never see or store your bank password
  • Included with Pause Family
Always there

Help at 10 p.m. on a Sunday, because that's when scams happen.

  • Ask Pause, a 24/7 assistant for "Is this real?" questions
  • Setup Sunday, free live video help every Sunday at 4 PM Eastern
  • Scam Weather, a 60-second tip every Monday on what's trending near you
  • Spot the Scam, a weekly drill with streaks to keep everyone sharp
