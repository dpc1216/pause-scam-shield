Questions

Before you choose.

What happens after my 5 free checks?

Your checks reset on the first of every month. Scam Radar, Scam Weather, and Ask Pause stay free, and you can upgrade at any time.

Can my son or daughter pay for my plan?

Yes. Most Family plans are bought by an adult child for a parent. One person pays, and up to six people are protected.

Will my Guardian see my messages?

No. Your Guardian is told only that a scam was blocked or a risky payment was flagged. Never your messages, calls, or balance.

Can I get Pause through my bank or employer?

That's the plan. Banks, credit unions, and employers can offer Pause Family to members for free. If yours does, enter their code in the app. See partner plans.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly plans can be canceled anytime. The yearly Family plan is $99, which saves $20.88 compared with paying monthly.
