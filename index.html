What Pause does

Four quiet protections that work in the moment before you act.

I.

The check

Share any text, email, link, QR code, or call. Get Safe, Be careful, or Scam, with the reasons in plain English.

II.

Family Circle

Choose a Guardian who is told only when something is high-risk. They never see your messages.

III.

Family Code Word

When a "grandchild in trouble" calls, Pause reminds you to ask for the word an AI voice copy can't know.

IV.

Money Guard

A read-only bank link that flags gift-card runs, first-time wires, and crypto ATMs before more money leaves.
