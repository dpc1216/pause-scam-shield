About Pause

Almost every scam works the same way. It rushes you.

We started Pause at the University of Miami after studying how attacks begin: not with clever code, but with pressure on a person. Scammers now write perfect texts and copy a loved one's voice, so "look for typos" no longer works. We are building the opposite of urgency.

Our plan

From founding members to our first bank partner in ten months.

A 30-day Kickstarter campaign with a $50,000 goal sells founding memberships and funds the first version of the app.

Jul 2027

Pause launches on the App Store and Google Play, starting in South Florida.

Oct 2027

Our first bank or credit union pilot, during Cybersecurity Awareness Month.

What we believe

Trust is the product.

  • No ads, and we never sell your data. A safety app that profits from your data is not a safety app.
  • Security before launch. We will complete a SOC 2 security audit and publish a clear privacy policy.
  • Independence for parents. Guardians are told about risk, never shown private messages.
  • Local first. We plan to run scam-awareness workshops with senior centers in Miami-Dade and groups older adults already trust, such as AARP's Fraud Watch Network.
